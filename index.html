About me

Engineering analysis grounded in practical testing

I am an MEng Aerospace Engineering graduate from the University of Surrey and a UK CAA RPC-L1 qualified Rotorcraft remote pilot. My experience combines aerodynamics, aircraft systems, structures, programming and data analysis with practical UAV development.

Through Team Peryton, I helped integrate and flight-test an autonomous fixed-wing aircraft using flight controllers, mission-planning software, telemetry and payload systems. I also analysed autonomous flight logs to investigate unexpected aircraft behaviour and support safer flight-test decisions.

I am seeking an early-career role where I can contribute to UAV engineering, testing or field operations while developing deeper experience in autonomous and BVLOS systems.
